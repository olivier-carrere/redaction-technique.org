---
title: "Project definition"
description: "Communicating technical information without knowing to whom or for what purpose is a futile effort."
---

A **technical writing** project adds value to products and helps the company to market its offering more effectively. But, as with R&D or marketing projects, project definition is the key to estimating budget and benefits.

Communicating technical information without knowing to whom or for what purpose is a futile effort. Before initiating a **technical writing** project, it is essential to clearly define it. In particular, you need to determine :

| Element | Description |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Increase company awareness, increase media coverage, encourage prospects to contact the company, reduce technical support costs... |
| General public, journalists, prospects, customers...                                                                                                   |
| White paper, instruction manual or user guide, brochure and *flyer*, *web* site, company magazine, *print* or *online*...                 |
| Depending on your field of activity, the project will be available in one or more languages (mainly English in the IT sector).
| The final document can be published on an Internet or Extranet site, sent as an attachment by *mail*, delivered in person in paper format, etc.

Analysis of the project results then provides valuable information for further improving the impact of subsequent projects.
