DITA XML enables significant productivity gains by reducing the volume of content that the technical writer creates, translates, and maintains. However, this productivity gain involves increased complexity, as explored in [structured and unstructured formats](../structured-vs-unstructured-formats/).

While DITA XML projects are more *complex*, they are still less *complicated* than projects based on more traditional formats like FrameMaker. This is because DITA XML follows a logical architecture. As a result, the technical writer can rely on the predictable behavior of the tools used, avoiding the *tricks and hacks* needed to work around bugs or the erratic behavior of more cumbersome tools.

**DITA XML is complex but predictable; traditional formats are complicated and erratic.**

The following table presents the different levels of complexity introduced by DITA XML and the solutions that help the technical writer manage them more effectively:

| Complexity                                                | Solution                                                                           |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| DITA XML syntax                                           | <abbr title="Integrated Development Environment">IDE</abbr> such as XMetaL or nXML |
| Relationship management between atomic information building blocks | A dedicated <abbr title="Content Management System">CMS</abbr> such as Componize or DocZone    |
| XSLT stylesheet syntax                                    | Graphical stylesheet creation software                                             |

For a small technical writing team, the primary challenge will be applying the company's visual identity to the output. Other aspects can be managed without specialized tools, through effective communication and a series of best practices — one of the most important being the [KISS principle of simplicity](../../costs/kiss-principle/).
