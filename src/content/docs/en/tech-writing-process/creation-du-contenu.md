<a id="content-creation"></a>

The **technical copywriter** creates the content of the **technical copywriting project** in constant dialogue with the company's various players: R&D departments, marketing. He or she takes into account the various constraints associated with the life cycle of technical writing materials.

In particular, the **technical writer** is responsible for:

- have the content validated by his or her contacts, so that any necessary changes can be made as early as possible; this guarantees that the result will be in line with the project initially defined,
- minimize the volume of source text and images to reduce production, maintenance and translation costs,
- take into account internationalization constraints right from the start of the project.
