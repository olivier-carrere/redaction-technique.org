---

In the case of a <abbr title="Content Management System">CMS</abbr> such as Drupal, Joomla or WordPress, the repository is an SQL database. It keeps a history, but only article by article, and does not allow text search and replacement across all content. This may justify the choice of managing the underlying content of the <abbr title="Content Management System">CMS</abbr> in the form of text files.
