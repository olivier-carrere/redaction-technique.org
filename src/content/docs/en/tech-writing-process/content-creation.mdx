<a id="content-creation"></a>

The technical writer creates the content for the technical writing project through continuous communication with various stakeholders within the company, such as R&D departments and marketing. They take into consideration the various constraints associated with the lifecycle of technical writing materials. This phase follows [information gathering](../gathering-information/) and leads into [validation and quality control](../validation-quality-control/).

In particular, the technical writer is responsible for:

- having the content validated by their contacts to ensure that any necessary changes can be made as early as possible. This ensures that the result aligns with the project's initial definition,
- minimizing the volume of source text and images to reduce production, maintenance, and translation costs,
- considering internationalization constraints from the outset of the project.
