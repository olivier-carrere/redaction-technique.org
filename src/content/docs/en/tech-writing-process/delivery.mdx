Delivery is the final step of the process: the validated content reaches its audiences in the format and through the channel chosen at [project definition](../project-definition/). The same source can surface as several different deliverables, for example:

- Animations published on streaming sites
- Brochures distributed at trade shows or provided to customers by sales engineers
- Newsletters sent to customers
- Updated website content
- Documents posted online as PDFs or distributed as printed guides, etc.
