Content is a company's intangible asset and must be protected accordingly. It can be managed in various repositories, such as directories, enterprise content management tools, and version management software. The question of [which repository suits group work](quel-referentiel-pour-le-travail-de-groupe/) is critical: [shared network directories](les-repertoires-reseau-partages-peu-adaptes-au-travail-de-groupe/) present significant limitations, while [version management systems](les-systemes-de-gestion-de-versions-rustiques-mais-fiables/) offer a far more reliable alternative.
