Content is a company's intangible asset and must be protected accordingly. It can be managed in various repositories, such as directories, enterprise content management tools, and version management software. The question of [which repository suits group work](../which-repository-for-group-work/) is critical: [shared network directories](../shared-network-directories/) present significant limitations, while [version management systems](../version-management-systems/) offer a far more reliable alternative.
